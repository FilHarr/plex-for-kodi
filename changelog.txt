[-0.1.0-]
Plex Uno (script.plexmod-uno): a reworked fork of PlexMod for Kodi that installs beside the stock add-on. Based on PlexMod 1.14.1 (2707bbe7).

- Install: own add-on ID; the first run copies the stock add-on's preferences and data, but not its sign-in
- Install: updates come from the Maybe Panic repository; the built-in updater is off
- Skin: a startup check offers the Plextuary Uno skin for the device (Plextuary, CoreELEC or CE custom builds) and its Inter 4 font set
- Navigation: a persistent sidebar replaces the top section bar - Home, Search, Watchlist, Playlists and the libraries you pick, from any server
- Navigation: detail screens open inside one host screen, Back steps through them the same way everywhere, and a sidebar click opens a section fresh
- Libraries: Recommended, Library, Categories and Collections tabs; sort options read from the server
- Home and Recommended: hub rows with a fixed focus position, previews of the rows above and below, and hero art and details for the focused item
- Home: rows and Continue Watching from every server, in Plex's order; Manage Hubs and a Libraries picker to arrange them
- Rows: the first 20 items and a See more grid of the rest
- Detail screens: Show, Episodes, Pre-play, Artist, Album, Person, Collections and Playlists rebuilt - clear logos, media info pills, shared button rows and colour panels taken from the art
- Person: a filmography from every server, plus Cast & Crew and credits from Plex Discover
- Search: a sidebar screen searching every server, results as a card grid with one entry per title
- Post-play: hub-style tiles, a text countdown and the options in-app
- Music: plays through real Plex play queues; rebuilt music player and play queue screens; repeat and skip fixed
- Servers: each item follows its own server; offline, back online and gone servers are handled, with a prompt to try again
- Playback: Stop always stops and the server decides what's watched; playback stops when the add-on exits (setting, on by default)
- Watched: ticks follow Plex's played state, through a re-watch
- Settings: "Ignore mouse and touch input" (System), on by default, since mouse and touch navigation isn't fully supported. This turns mouse/touch off for existing users on update
- Settings: the Theme option is gone; the modern-colored theme is always used
- Look: Inter 4 fonts, rounded posters with matching shadows and focus rings, art-tinted backgrounds without banding
- Performance: faster screen opens, fewer GUI calls, and leaked screens and signal handlers fixed
